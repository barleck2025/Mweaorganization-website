DUPC3 Waterpans Project

A 5-year sustainable waterpans project (2023-2027) funded by the Dutch government, focused on water and health. The project builds local capacities in Tanzania and Kenya through collaborative activities such as joint research, education, and knowledge sharing, ensuring communities develop sustainable solutions to their water-related challenges.

2023 - 2027 Tanzania & Kenya Water & Health

Women and Water for Change (WWCC)

Funded by the Coca-Cola Foundation and implemented by IHE Delft, this 18-month project (2019-2021) focused on strengthening women's participation in decision-making related to water, public health, and sustainable resource management. MWEA led the implementation in the Mara wetlands through a sub-grant from IHE Delft.

2019 - 2021 Women Empowerment 3 Districts

Vocational Training & Skills Development

Skills are the foundation of economic independence. Our vocational training program teaches women and youth practical skills in tailoring, agriculture, handicrafts, and small business management, enabling them to create sustainable livelihoods.
