Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about MWEA.

How can I support MWEA?

You can support us through donations, volunteering, or partnerships. Use the form above or contact us to learn more.

Where does my donation go?

100% of your donation goes directly to our programs - education sponsorships, water projects, vocational training, and community development initiatives. We maintain full transparency.

Can I volunteer with MWEA?

Yes! We welcome volunteers with various skills - teaching, mentoring, fundraising, and more. Contact us using the form above to discuss how you can contribute.

Where does MWEA operate?

MWEA operates primarily in the Mara region of Tanzania, covering districts such as Butiama, Tarime, and Rorya, with plans for expansion into other Lake Zone areas.

Mara Women Empowerment Assistance

Empowering women, youth, and communities in Tanzania's Mara region through education, economic support, and sustainable development since 2004.
