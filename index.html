Who We Are

Mara Women Empowerment Assistance (MWEA) is a grassroots NGO registered in Tanzania in 2004, dedicated to empowering women, youth, and communities.

Our Mission

To empower communities through innovative microfinance services, community development projects, and capacity-building initiatives while advocating for gender equality and inclusive economic growth.

Learn More

Our Vision

A self-sustaining, equitable society where every individual, particularly marginalized groups, can contribute to and benefit from economic growth and environmental conservation.

Learn More

Our Values

Transparency, accountability, respect, community focus, responsiveness, effectiveness, efficiency, and reliability guide everything we do.

Learn More

What We Do

Our programs address the most pressing needs in the Mara region.

Economic Empowerment

Micro-loans, entrepreneurship training, and SACCOS support to help individuals start and grow small businesses.

Learn More

Education Support

Scholarships, school supplies, and infrastructure support for underprivileged children from primary to university level.

Learn More

Water Projects

Constructing water pans and promoting sustainable water resource management for communities in need.

Learn More

Community Well-being

Health services, HIV/AIDS awareness, food security, and nutrition programs for vulnerable populations.

Learn More

Environmental Conservation

Tree nurseries, beekeeping, poultry farming, and sustainable agriculture initiatives across the Mara region.

Learn More

Advocacy & Education

Raising awareness on critical social issues, promoting gender equality, and fighting Gender-Based Violence.
